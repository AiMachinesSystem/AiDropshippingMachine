# Profitability vs competition — Amazon-sourced niches (2026-06-17)

> Read-only. No listing/import/publish. Every datum labeled; the verifier confirmed **no fabricated ASIN/price/review data** (10 ASINs + 2 eBay sold-pages spot-checked, all matched).

## 0. The blunt truth on margin (read first)
**Amazon → eBay arbitrage does NOT clear 50% net. The verified ceiling is ~30–42% net** (after FVF 13.6% + $0.40, before inbound shipping) — because Amazon's own price already sits part-way up toward the eBay price. The "60–101% markup over cost" you'll see is **not** 50% margin: markup-on-cost ≠ margin-on-sale. So:
- **If the goal is >50% margin → Amazon sourcing can't get you there**; that needs cheaper sourcing (AliExpress/CJ) or your own bundles.
- **If the goal is to out-profit eBay competitors using Amazon → it's real, at ~30–42% net**, and only on the specific patterns below.

## 1. How to be MORE profitable than the competition (the 6 levers)
1. **Sell the DIFFERENTIATED BUNDLE, never the commodity floor.** Every winning niche has a cheap-loop/single-item floor (race-to-bottom) AND a thin mid-ticket "complete kit / with-stand / with-bins / structured" band. List the bundle; ignore the floor. *(This is exactly the account's proven-winner pattern.)*
2. **Win only on a REAL cost gap — verified both ways.** Out-profit competitors only where Amazon's **no-name** price sits **well below** the prevailing eBay price. Where Amazon's price is close to eBay's (e.g., inflatable dog ramp $128 vs $199 → only ~22%), the edge isn't there via Amazon.
3. **Lead with social proof you can mirror.** Pick Amazon products with deep reviews (600–29,000) — higher conversion + you can reference quality. (Don't copy their photos/brand — VeRO.)
4. **Scale on VERIFIED demand (eBay "X sold" badges), not watchers.** Only push budget where a live listing already shows real sold counts.
5. **Avoid VeRO and weight — they silently kill margin.** Reject branded/trademarked items (ThermoPro, URPOWER, Furhaven, Goodyear, etc.) and bulky/heavy goods (inflatable platforms, dumbbells) where dimensional shipping eats the spread.
6. **Undercut the BRANDED cluster, not the generic floor.** The profit sits between the no-name floor and the branded premium: source no-name, list just under the branded band (e.g., pond aerator: branded $110–139 → you at $119–129 on a $70 Amazon cost).

> ⚠️ **Policy note (recurring):** Amazon→eBay is **retail arbitrage**, which conflicts with eBay's policy and is the structural risk in the existing catalog. Owner-directed to use Amazon; flagged. Sourcing the same items from AliExpress/CJ would both improve margin AND remove this risk.

## 3. The profit playbook (apply to every new listing)
1. Confirm a **verified eBay sold badge** on a comparable listing (demand is real).
2. Confirm the **Amazon no-name price is ≥40% below** the prevailing eBay sell (the gap).
3. List the **bundle/complete/with-X variant**, priced **just under the branded cluster**.
4. **Generic title + own photos** (no brand, no "compatible-with <trademark>") → VeRO-safe.
5. Light-to-ship only; reject bulky/heavy.
6. Expect **~30–42% net**, not 50% — model the business on that.
