Stop the Wet Mess Behind Your Tap

Tired of a constantly wet, dripping countertop around the base of your faucet? This soft silicone mat sits behind the tap and catches every splash, drip and overflow, then channels the water straight back into the sink so your counter stays dry and tidy.

Why You'll Love It

  • Catches splashes & drips - keeps the area behind your faucet dry instead of pooling water.
  • Self-draining design - the raised edge and slope guide water back into the basin.
  • Doubles as a drying tray - rest your sponge, soap, scrubber or brush on top.
  • Flexible food-grade silicone - bends to fit around the faucet base; easy to trim if needed.
  • Wipe-clean & reusable - rinse under the tap or pop in the dishwasher; no more soggy cloths.
  • Non-slip & quiet - grips the counter and protects the surface from scratches and water stains.

Fits Almost Any Sink

Works in the kitchen, bathroom, laundry room, bar, RV or office kitchenette. The slim, flexible mat sits flush against most standard faucets.
