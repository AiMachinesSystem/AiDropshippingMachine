Keep Your Sink Tidy - No Tools, No Holes

Reclaim your countertop with a sturdy stainless steel sink caddy that holds your sponge, dish brush, and scrubber all in one spot. Peel, stick, and you're done - no drilling, no screws, no damage to your sink or wall.

  • No-drill adhesive install - strong waterproof backing sticks to most smooth sinks, tile, or glass; ideal for renters and anyone who hates power tools.
  • Built-in drain tray - water runs off so sponges and brushes air-dry fast, helping prevent that damp, grimy smell.
  • Rustproof stainless steel - built to handle a wet kitchen day after day without flaking or corroding.
  • Space-saving 2-in-1 design - sponge slot plus brush holder keeps clutter off the counter and everything within reach.

Tip: for the strongest hold, clean and fully dry the surface before applying, then press firmly and wait before loading it up.
