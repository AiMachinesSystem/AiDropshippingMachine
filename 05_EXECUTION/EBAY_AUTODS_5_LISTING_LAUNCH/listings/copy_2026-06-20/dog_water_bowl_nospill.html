Keep Floors Dry - No More Sloshing Water

Stop water from splashing out of the bowl in the car, kitchen, or crate. A free-floating disk sits on the water surface and presses down spills before they happen - so your dog drinks calmly and your floors stay dry.

  • Floating disk stops splashing: the disk rises and falls as your dog drinks, blocking water from sloshing over the edge during travel or play.
  • Slows fast drinkers: the disk meters each sip, helping dogs that gulp too quickly and reducing mess around the bowl.
  • Stainless steel build: rust-resistant, easy to rinse, and made to last - no electronics, no batteries, no pump to fail.
  • Non-slip base: stays put on smooth floors and in the car instead of sliding around.
  • Easy to clean: lift out the disk and rinse - simple, non-electric design.

Great for: messy drinkers, road trips, crates, kitchens, and homes that hate wet floors. Works for dogs and cats.

Hand wash recommended.
