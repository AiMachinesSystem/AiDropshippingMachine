Know the Water Is Just Right Before Bath Time

Take the guesswork out of every bath. This floating thermometer gives you a fast, easy read of the water temperature so you can keep bath time safe and comfortable for your little one.

  • Floats in the water - sits right on the surface so you can check the temperature at a glance.
  • Waterproof design - built to live in the tub and handle daily splashes.
  • Easy-to-read display - clear temperature reading the moment you place it in the water.
  • Compact and lightweight - small enough to keep by the tub and simple to store.
  • Doubles as a fun bath companion - a friendly shape that keeps baby curious during bath time.

How to Use

Place the thermometer in the bath water, wait a moment, and read the temperature before lowering your baby in. Rinse and air dry after use.

For parental convenience only. Always test bath water yourself and never leave a child unattended in or near water.
