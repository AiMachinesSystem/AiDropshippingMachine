# Launch Prep

Turns a validated niche + owner decisions into a launch-ready pack.
**v1.0 (2026-06-13).** Internal drafting only — every external/live step is
an explicit GO gate for the owner. GO/NO-GO always stays with the owner.
**v1.1 (cockpit auto-refresh)** — AUTO-REFRESH cockpit obbligatorio
al close-out (MASTER_DASHBOARD + NEXT_ACTIONS), stessa forza della regola
indice.

## INPUTS (check before anything)

1. **Niche** — must have a registered block in `RESEARCH_MEMORY_INDEX.md`
   (a niche-validation + competitor/ads run). No block → STOP and recommend
   `niche-intelligence-run` first; do not improvise market claims.
2. **Owner decisions** — read what the owner has already decided (product
   provenance/rights, name preferences, budget, geo). Anything not decided
   is listed as an OPEN DECISION, never assumed.

## BLOCKER #1 — PRODUCT PROVENANCE / RIGHTS (gate zero)

Without an explicit owner statement of (a) WHERE the plans/products come
from and (b) WHAT rights/licenses permit selling them, the skill outputs
ONLY: the pack skeleton, the sourcing-options table (from research, with
risks), and the decision request — then **STOPS**. No brand assets, no
policy drafts, no test plan beyond placeholders. Rationale: every claim in
the trust thesis depends on provenance being real (the market's documented
failure mode is fabricated provenance/proof — never replicate).

## PACK STRUCTURE (when gate zero is answered)

1. **Brand shortlist** — 8–12 names with: positioning fit, pronounceability
   in target locales, RDAP domain availability check (**read-only — NEVER
   register**), trademark-collision quick SERP check [OBSERVED-labeled],
   social-handle visual check (public pages only).
2. **Offer structure** — hero SKU + ladder with price points justified by
   the niche's observed bands; **anchors only with a declared, real basis**
   (or no compare-at at all); real bonus stack (each bonus must exist before
   it is claimed); volume claims must match the actual catalog count.
3. **Policies** — refund/guarantee drafted around a REAL guarantee the owner
   commits to (no-questions-asked class preferred; EU withdrawal handled
   honestly); plain-language wording; legal entity visible. Flag for owner
   legal review — drafts are not legal advice.
4. **Technical checklist (Shopify/Meta)** — store setup items (theme, review
   app from day one, analytics/pixel wiring, locale structure, email
   capture), Meta Business Manager prerequisites (domain verification, pixel,
   page), each item marked INTERNAL-DRAFT vs **GO-GATED (live action — owner
   executes or explicitly authorizes)**.
5. **First test plan** — per the niche's evidence: hook families to test
   (e.g. trust-differentiation vs proven pain hook), budget structure
   placeholder (owner sets numbers), success metrics defined BEFORE spend,
   duplication-testing structure, measurement plan. All spend = GO gate.
6. **GO gates list** — explicit, per-action: domain registration · account
   creation · store publication · pixel/page creation · any spend · any
   outreach. Approval never carries over between gates.

## EVIDENCE & SAFETY (binding)

Every market claim cites the registered runs (file + date) or is labeled
[INFERRED]/[UNKNOWN]. No invented numbers, no unverifiable claims in any
draft copy — draft claims must each carry their substantiation source
(claims without one are marked NOT USABLE). Fabricated-proof tactics are
anti-patterns, never templates. Read-only web. Nothing live without GO.

## OUTPUT

One file:
`05_EXECUTION/<NICHE-SLUG>/YYYY-MM-DD_LAUNCH_PACK_v N.md` (new niches get
their own folder — NEVER write into another project's execution folder).
Standard close: decisions taken vs OPEN DECISIONS (owner) · GO gates ·
blockers · files updated. Register the pack in `RESEARCH_MEMORY_INDEX.md`
(niche block, `launch-prep` line) and in `00_SYSTEM_CONTROL/BACKLOG.md` if
items remain open.
**AUTO-REFRESH cockpit (v1.1, stessa forza della regola indice):** after
registration, update `00_SYSTEM_CONTROL\MASTER_DASHBOARD.md` (ultimi run ·
stato progetto · decisioni pendenti) + `00_SYSTEM_CONTROL\NEXT_ACTIONS.md`;
open decisions become notes in `00_SYSTEM_CONTROL\DECISIONS\` (status:
pending) and owner actions become task notes in `00_SYSTEM_CONTROL\TASKS\`.
**No cockpit refresh = pack INCOMPLETE.**
